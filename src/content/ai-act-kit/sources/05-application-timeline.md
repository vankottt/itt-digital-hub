# Dates of application

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 113, consolidated 27.07.2026.
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27

Commission overview (context, not a substitute for Article 113):
https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai

## Article 113 (extract)

This Regulation entered into force on the twentieth day following its publication in the Official Journal.

It shall apply from **2 August 2026**.

However:

- Chapters I and II shall apply from **2 February 2025**, with the exception of Article 5(1), first subparagraph, points (ba) and (bb), and Article 5(1a) and (1b), which shall apply from **2 December 2026**;
- Chapter III Section 4, Chapter V, Chapter VII and Chapter XII and Article 78 shall apply from **2 August 2025**, with the exception of Article 101;
- Chapter III, Sections 1, 2 and 3, with the exception of Article 6(5), shall apply from:
  - **2 December 2027** as regards AI systems classified as high-risk pursuant to Article 6(2) and Annex III; and
  - **2 August 2028** as regards AI systems classified as high-risk pursuant to Article 6(1) and Annex I;
- Articles 102 to 110 shall apply from **27 July 2026**.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

## Reading for this assistant

- AI literacy (Chapter I includes Article 4) and prohibited practices (Chapter II) started earlier than the general 2 August 2026 date, with the exceptions written above.
- High-risk system duties in Chapter III Sections 1–3 follow the later dates in the consolidated text. Do not tell a small firm that full high-risk product obligations already apply on an earlier date if Article 113 says otherwise.
- Always check whether the user’s question is about literacy, prohibitions, GPAI provider duties, or high-risk product rules. They do not share one date.
